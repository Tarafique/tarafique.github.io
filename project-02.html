Project notes

Linux-based sensor acquisition

A proof of concept for sensor collection, live display and CSV logging on a Raspberry Pi.

Project stageInitial proof of concept completed
RolePrototyping and integration

Context

The initial prototype combined GPS and IMU inputs into a data stream that could be displayed during operation and saved for analysis.

Work included

  • GPS and motion-sensor integration on a Raspberry Pi-based platform.
  • Calibration and sample-timing checks.
  • Real-time data visualization through an initial interface.
  • CSV logging for later review.

Acquisition path

GPS / IMUSensors
Raspberry PiAcquisition
CSV / UIMonitoring

Linux scope

The Linux-related work was application-level prototyping and hardware interfacing on Raspberry Pi. It is not a claim of kernel-driver, bootloader or custom distribution development.

Observations

Timestamps, sample consistency, calibration state and logging format are important alongside the dashboard itself. These details were considered while developing the acquisition workflow.

Status

An initial proof of concept was developed. A later compact custom-hardware architecture was under consideration, but it is not represented as a completed board.
